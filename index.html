Tips aur modes
  • Auto Split by default fast engine (hardware encoder, parallel clips) use karta hai. Live mode on ho to video play hote hue real-time record hoti hai (slow, lekin pause/resume kaam karta hai).
  • Quick Split Exact: seconds, aspect ratio, rotate aur captions sab apply hote hain. Speed aap ke PC ke encoder par depend karti hai, status mein asli speed dikhti hai.
  • Quick Split Instant: lossless keyframe cut, bohat tez, lekin clip lengths 1-8 sec upar neeche ho sakti hain aur ratio Original rehta hai. Sirf 90/180/270 rotate chalta hai.
  • Captions on hon to Instant mode use nahi hota. Dono Quick modes H.264 MP4/MOV ke liye hain.
  • Split ke dauran is tab ko open aur visible rakhein.
