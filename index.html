Pehle video select karein.

Split ke dauran video pause karein to splitting bhi ruk jati hai, aur dobara play karne par wahin se continue hoti hai. Is tab ko open aur visible rakhein. Auto Split ab by default fast engine (hardware encoder, parallel clips) use karta hai. Live mode on karein to video play hote hue real-time record hoti hai (slow, lekin preview aur pause kaam karte hain). Quick Split bina play kiye clips banata hai. Mode 'Exact' mein seconds aur aspect ratio dono apply hoti hain (har frame hardware encoder se guzarta hai, to speed PC par depend karti hai; status mein asli speed dikhti hai). Captions on hon to clips Exact ya Live mode mein hi bantay hain (Instant mode captions nahi laga sakta). Rotate buttons video ko kisi bhi degree par ghumate hain (Instant mode sirf 90/180/270 par chalta hai, custom degree ke liye Exact use hota hai). Mode 'Instant' lossless keyframe cut karta hai: bijli jaisa tez, lekin lengths 1-8 sec upar neeche ho sakti hain aur ratio Original rehta hai. Dono H.264 MP4/MOV ke liye hain.
